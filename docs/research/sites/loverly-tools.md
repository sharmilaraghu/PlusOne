## Wedding Planning Tools FAQ

### Are Loverly's Wedding Planning Tools Really Free?

Yes. The checklist, guest list, address collector, budget tracker and vendor manager are all free, with no premium tier, trial or hidden fee. The one paid product is the wedding website, which is built on Squarespace and needs a paid annual Squarespace plan.

### What Are the Best Wedding Planning Tools to Start With?

Start with the [wedding checklist](https://loverly.com/tools/wedding-checklist/setup) so you know what to do and when, then build your [guest list](https://loverly.com/tools/guest-list/setup) and set a [budget](https://loverly.com/tools/budget-tracker/setup). Those three drive nearly every other decision, from venue size to vendor spend.

### Do I Need an Account to Use the Tools?

Yes. A free account keeps your checklist, guests, budget and vendors together so each tool can use the others — your guest count feeds your budget, and your vendors show up on your checklist.

### Can I Plan a Wedding on My Phone with These Tools?

Yes. Every tool works in your phone's browser with no app download, so you can update your guest list or tick off a task wherever you are.

### How Does the Guest List Collect Addresses?

Share one link from the [wedding address collector](https://loverly.com/tools/wedding-address-collector/setup). Guests enter their own mailing address and it lands straight in your guest list, ready for save-the-dates and invitations.

### How Is Loverly Different from Zola or the Knot?

Zola and The Knot pair their planning tools with a registry and vendor marketplace. Loverly's tools are free and independent of any registry or vendor listing. See our full [Zola vs The Knot comparison](https://loverly.com/planning/wedding-101/zola-vs-the-knot).
