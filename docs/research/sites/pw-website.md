## Our Story

Upload image

Our story began in the magic desert of Black Rock City, where the air was thick with magic of "playa provide". Across the space of temple, our eyes met, and in that instant, the world seemed to fade. A glance became a spark, pulling us closer until the night dissolved into deep conversation, laughter, and the intoxicating realization of how much we shared. That spark grew into something extraordinary — something neither of us could ignore.

From that night forward, our lives intertwined in a journey of discovery. Together, we’ve wandered through the icy tundras of Siberia, ascended Nepal’s towering peaks, meandered through Morocco’s vibrant souks, and found peace in Japan’s serene landscapes. With every step, we’ve learned that home isn’t a place — it’s the way we feel in each other’s presence, exploring the world hand in hand.

Three years later, we’re more than just a couple. We are best friends, soulmates, and fearless partners in this beautiful, chaotic adventure of life. Every moment — every challenge and every joy — has drawn us closer, and now, we’re ready to begin the next chapter.

We can’t wait to celebrate our love with you as we make it official!
