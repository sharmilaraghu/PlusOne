Where the AI does the work

PlusOne uses language models for the reading and writing a wedding generates. They work from your plan and from what vendors actually wrote; a price or an email address the page does not state is left blank, not guessed.

PlusOne never books, pays or signs. Anything that is yours to decide comes back to you as one clear question.

  • Reads each vendor's own website into a card with prices, packages and a source link.
  • Ranks the vendors it found and gives a one-sentence reason for each.
  • Drafts the inquiries and follow-ups, and answers the simple questions vendors ask.
  • Reads replies and attached PDFs into quotes: total, deposit and what is included.
  • Reads a guest's reply into an RSVP, a headcount and any allergies.
  • Answers questions about your plan, and proposes next steps that run only when you press the button.
